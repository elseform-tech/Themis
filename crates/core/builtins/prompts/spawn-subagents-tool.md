Start subagents for independent parts of the task. Tell the person first, in your reply text, who you are starting and what each one will do. Each one runs on its own with only its brief, and is read-only unless you give it `write_paths`: project-relative folders (ending in `/`) or files, never globs, that it alone may change. Two running subagents can't share a path, and `.themis/` and `.git/` are never allowed. The brief must be self-contained: what to look at, what to report, and in what form. This returns their ids at once and you keep working. Each subagent's result arrives later as a note, before your next step; call wait_subagents only when you have nothing else to do. A subagent that got something wrong can be corrected with message_subagent rather than started again. Good uses: reviewing or investigating several areas at once, searches across a large codebase. Give write paths for parallel work on separate areas, such as tests in one folder and docs in another. Not for a single small edit or anything that needs the conversation.
