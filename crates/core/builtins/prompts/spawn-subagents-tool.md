Start subagents for independent parts of the task. Each one runs on its own, read-only, with only its brief, so the brief must be self-contained: what to look at, what to report, and in what form. This returns their ids at once and you keep working. Each subagent's result arrives later as a note, before your next step; call wait_subagents only when you have nothing else to do. Good uses: reviewing or investigating several areas at once, searches across a large codebase. Not for a single small edit or anything that needs the conversation.
