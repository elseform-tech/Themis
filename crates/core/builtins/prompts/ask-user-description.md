Ask the user without stopping your work. Give your answer in `recommended` with a one-line `why`: one of 2 to 4 `choices`, or alone as a suggestion for an open question. Every choice gives a short `answer` and a `hint`: one plain paragraph (no list) saying what you will do if it is picked, its advantages and disadvantages, its consequences (including whether it is hard to undo) and anything else the user needs to decide without guessing. Keep working on your recommendation; the answer arrives as a message. Set `blocking` (no `recommended`) only when you cannot suggest any answer and nothing more can be done without it. One thing per question; reuse `topic` to update an open one. To ask several related things at once, put up to 4 questions in `questions` (each with these same fields) instead of `question`: the user sees them as pages of one box and their answers arrive together in one message.
