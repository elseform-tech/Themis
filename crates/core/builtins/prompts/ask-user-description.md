Ask the user without stopping your work. Give your answer in `recommended` with a one-line `why`: one of 2 to 4 `choices`, or alone as a suggestion for an open question. Every choice gives a short `answer`, `does` (what you will do if it is picked: the steps, commands or files), `advantages`, `disadvantages` and `consequences` (what follows from it, and whether it is hard to undo), so the user can decide without guessing. Keep working on your recommendation; the answer arrives as a message. Set `blocking` (no `recommended`) only when you cannot suggest any answer and nothing more can be done without it. One thing per question; reuse `topic` to update an open one. To ask several related things at once, put up to 4 questions in `questions` (each with these same fields) instead of `question`: the user sees them as pages of one box and their answers arrive together in one message.
