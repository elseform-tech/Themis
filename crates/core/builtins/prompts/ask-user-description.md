Ask the user without stopping your work. Give your answer in `recommended` with a one-line `why`: one of 2 to 4 `choices`, or alone as a suggestion for an open question. Every choice gives a short `answer` and a `hint`: one plain paragraph (no list) of two to four sentences, at least 160 characters, that says what you will do if it is picked, what it gains, what it costs or risks (name a real downside for every choice, the recommended one too), and what follows from it, including whether it is hard to undo, so the user can decide without guessing. Every choice is a real answer: the box always has a field for the user's own words, so never add one that only invites them ("Other", "Something else", "I have a preference, tell me"). Keep working on your recommendation; the answer arrives as a message. Set `blocking` (no `recommended`) only when you cannot suggest any answer and nothing more can be done without it. One thing per question; reuse `topic` to update an open one. To ask several related things at once, put up to 4 questions in `questions` (each with these same fields) instead of `question`: the user sees them as pages of one box and their answers arrive together in one message.
