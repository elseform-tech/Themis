VSOP87 data files
=================

Each file holds series for one body. A series starts with a header line:
  col 2-7 'VSOP87', col 42 the variable (1=L, 2=B, 3=R for version D),
  col 60 the power k of T, col 61-67 the number of terms.
Each term line has fixed columns:
  col 2-5 body and version code, col 6 variable, col 7 power, col 8-12 index,
  col 13-48 twelve multipliers of the planetary arguments (unused here),
  col 80-97 A amplitude (au or rad), col 98-111 B phase (rad),
  col 112-131 C frequency (rad per Julian millennium).
A variable is sum over k of T^k * sum(A * cos(B + C*T)), with
T = (JDE - 2451545.0) / 365250 in Julian millennia (TT).
Version D gives heliocentric L, B (rad) and R (au) for the ecliptic and equinox of date.
Precision: truncated series as shipped give about 1 arcsecond for 1900-2100.
