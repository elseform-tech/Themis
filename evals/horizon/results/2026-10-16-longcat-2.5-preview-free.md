## Reading (benchmark audit, 3 trials, 12k-token budget)

- **Runtime stops, not memory, ended 10 of 17 runs.** 7 runs ended on "tool batch exceeds the bounded
  delivery allowance": at a 12k budget a batch of 6+ parallel reads leaves under 1 KB per result,
  and the run fails instead of telling the agent. 3 ended on "Repeated unchanged tool results":
  re-reading files whose content was compacted away counts as a loop.
- **Plan persistence: 15 of 17 checkpoints with a saved plan carried it.** Both misses are one
  bug: in modules-plan-asked trial 2 an unchanged 692-byte plan vanished at checkpoints 3 and 4.
  The ledger is shrunk by halving its allowance until the checkpoint fits, and the plan line
  must fit in that allowance. Later checkpoints only restore from the previous one, so the plan
  stayed lost until the agent saved a new one.
- **Stories:** plan-asked passed 3/3 (one checkpoint each). Every failed stories run died on the
  batch stop, so the arms differ mostly in how the agent batched reads, not in what it remembered.
- **Modules:** no run passed. The completed runs kept the prompt-only rule in all 16 files and
  restated it, but the final total was wrong (58 and 62, not 64): per-module counts from before
  a checkpoint were lost. Agents read all modules before writing, then re-read ~16 after
  compaction.
